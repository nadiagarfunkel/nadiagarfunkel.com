About Nadia Garfunkel

I’m an entrepreneur and university educator at Albert School, where I teach marketing and sales.

I’ve worked across continents in tech startups, schools, and non-profit organizations making tech more accessible for non-technical people. I previously led go-to-market for imagi and consulted for the UN-backed Private Sector Humanitarian Alliance. Today I help early-stage teams with campaigns and ops, and I mentor students through career moves.

I gravitate toward work that’s inclusive and a little experimental, centered on connecting with people. Check out my article on innovative pedagogy.

Columbia and McGill shaped how I think and teach. English and Mandarin are my native languages, and my French is a solid B1. A US citizen living in Paris, I work independently as an auto-entrepreneur.
